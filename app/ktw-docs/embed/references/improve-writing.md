# Improve Writing

Refine text into professional academic and scientific language while preserving the original meaning.

## Purpose

Transform draft or informal text into polished, professional academic language with improved clarity, coherence, and formal tone.

## Scope

- **Input**: any text (draft paragraph, essay section, abstract, paper excerpt, report, or any prose).
- **Output**: refined text in place + list of changes made.

## Process

### Step 1 — Refine the text

Apply the following improvements directly to the text:

- Fix grammatical errors.
- Improve clarity and coherence.
- Convert to formal academic voice.
- Use common, easy-to-understand words and phrases — avoid obscure jargon where a simpler word suffices.
- Eliminate wordy sentences.
- Remove trivial statements.
- Avoid repeating the same words and phrases.
- Maintain the original meaning and intent throughout.

### Step 2 — Output

Produce two sections:

1. **Refined text** — the improved version, applied directly to the document in place.
2. **List of changes** — a summary of all modifications made to the original text, describing each change and why it was made.
