# Review guide — where are we

**What it answers:** the module's state of play for a manager: where it
stands on each review dimension, what changed, what to do next — the
synthesis of its current reviews.

## Read

The module's current reviews: every `review` under it, the newest of each
type (and the one before it, to see what moved), their ratings, summaries,
reports and open findings; the module's goals and features (what it is for);
its open requests (what is already planned).

## Judge

1. **By dimension** — for each dimension of its reviews: the worst rating,
   what drives it (the open critical and high findings), how it moved since
   the previous reviews.
2. **Coverage** — the dimensions with no review, or a review older than the
   module's last changes: a finding (`info` or `low`) asking for it.
3. **Priorities** — the five things to do first, each tied to the findings it
   closes, already planned (a request) or not.
4. **Re-verify** — never repeat a finding of another review without checking
   it is still true in the module as it is; drop the stale ones.

## Rate

The worst rating among the current reviews, lowered to `needs-attention` when
a dimension that applies has no review. Score: the mean of the current
reviews' scores, when they have one.

## Report

A table of dimensions (dimension, rating, trend, key finding), then the top
priorities in order (with the review and request each comes from), the
strengths, the dimensions to review next. Findings: only what this synthesis
adds (unreviewed dimensions, priorities with no request) — the other reviews'
findings stay theirs.
